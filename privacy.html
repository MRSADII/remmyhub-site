Payments are processed by third-party providers (Whop, Skrill, Paddle, or cryptocurrency networks). We never see or store your full card details.

We do not sell or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data is shared only with payment processors (to complete your purchase) and Discord (only if you voluntarily redeem a key there), solely to operate the service.
